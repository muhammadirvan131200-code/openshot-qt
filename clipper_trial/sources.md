## Editorial selection for the first 60-second trial
Use the broad theme **consumption vs. active practice**. Raymond Chin's AI-learning content supplies the skills/AI angle; Timothy Ronald's self-development/mentality themes supply a creator reference; Pertajam Pola Pikir supplies a science-and-focus angle. These creators are references for topic selection only in this prototype, not sources of downloaded audio/video.

## Verification and rights checklist
- Verify the exact original upload and current view count on the creator's channel before publication.
- Obtain permission or use source material under a license that permits clipping and redistribution.
- Check the applicable platform policy, music rights, and any third-party image/footage licenses.
- Keep a record of the original URL, source timecodes, license/permission and edit notes.
- Never use a reupload as proof of the creator's original content.
